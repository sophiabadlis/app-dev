# My Favorite Movie

## The Clique (2008)

**The Clique** is one of my favorite movies because it shows friendship, popularity, fashion, and the challenges of fitting in.

### About the Movie

The Clique is about a girl named Claire Lyons who moves to a new town and tries to fit in with a popular group of girls called the Pretty Committee.

### Why I Like It

1. Interesting storyline
2. Entertaining characters
3. Beautiful fashion and outfits
4. Lessons about friendship and acceptance

### Main Characters

- Massie Block
- Claire Lyons
- Alicia Rivera
- Dylan Marvil
- Kristen Gregory

### My Favorite Character

**Massie Block** is my favorite character because she is confident, fashionable, ambitious, and has a strong personality. I also like her sense of style and how she always stands out.

### My Rating

**10/10** – A fun and entertaining movie!

`Comedy` | `Teen Film` | `Drama`
